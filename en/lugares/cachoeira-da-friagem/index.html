Friagem Waterfall (Cachoeira da Friagem)

The monitored Friagem trail starts beyond the built-up area at the end of Alameda dos Pássaros in Siriúba. The route takes 1.5–2 hours through forest and open viewpoints to the approximately 25-meter waterfall. A guide is mandatory.

Highlights

  • Guide mandatory
  • 1.5–2 hour route
  • Forest and open sections
  • São Sebastião Channel views

How to get there

End of Alameda dos Pássaros, Siriúba; guide mandatory

Responsible travel

Bring drinking water and insect repellent.
