Poço Fundo Waterfall

Poço Fundo Waterfall is near the Bonete community. According to the municipality, a marked trail takes about 10 minutes from the beach. Reaching Bonete itself requires a long hike from Sepituba or a boat trip, subject to sea conditions. Do not confuse the short local walk with the time and effort required to reach Bonete.

Highlights

  • About 10 minutes on foot from Bonete Beach
  • Reach Bonete by long hike or boat
  • Marked final path through forest
  • Check currents and water depth before bathing

How to get there

First reach Bonete by trail or boat, then follow the local walking path

Responsible travel

Never assume pools are safe for diving; check the current and respect the local community.
