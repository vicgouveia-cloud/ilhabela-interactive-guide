Enchova Beach (Praia da Enchova)

One of the wildest and most pristine coastal expanses in southeastern Brazil. Located past Bonete, a clean river forms a stunning emerald lagoon right by the ocean.

Highlights

  • 100% untouched deserted wilderness
  • Emerald freshwater river lagoon
  • Ocean-polished pebble coastlines
  • Premier fishing & dive spot

How to get there

Trail from Praia do Bonete or boat

Responsible travel

Completely remote location. Pack all essentials and leave zero trace.
