Heart Overlook (Mirante do Coração)

The Heart Lookout is at the right-hand end of Castelhanos Beach when facing the ocean. After reaching the beach with authorized transport or by boat, follow the short trail and staircase uphill. The municipality reported renovations in 2024, including suspended steps and a metal-floor lookout deck with open grilles. The climb involves steps and a slope, so do not assume step-free accessibility.

Highlights

  • Famous heart-shaped bay view
  • Approach from the right-hand end of the beach
  • Renovated staircase and metal lookout deck
  • Arrange transport to Castelhanos first

How to get there

Reach Castelhanos by authorized 4x4, organized tour or boat; follow the beach to the right when facing the sea

Responsible travel

Stay on the staircase and designated deck. Avoid wet steps and exposed edges, and check Castelhanos access conditions.
