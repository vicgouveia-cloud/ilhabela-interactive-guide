Curral Beach

The most famous beach on the island for leisure and comfort. Features upscale beach lounges, gentle emerald waters, and Ilhabela most celebrated sunset scene.

Highlights

  • Spectacular sunsets over the channel
  • Refined beach clubs & live music
  • Calm waters for swimming & SUP
  • Charming seaside chapel

How to get there

Car, city bus or taxi via Av. José Pacheco do Nascimento
