Bonete Beach

An eco-sanctuary inhabited by a traditional caiçara fishing community. Famous for powerful surf barrels, emerald waters, golden sand, and a fresh river flowing right into the ocean.

Highlights

  • World-class surf breaks
  • Warm caiçara hospitality
  • Freshwater river estuary
  • Cinematic sunsets

How to get there

Trail from Sepituba or boat / Boneteiro
