Toca Waterfall (Cachoeira da Toca)

Toca Waterfall lies on the privately owned Fazenda da Toca near the start of the Castelhanos road. It is known for a grotto and a natural rock slide reportedly about 50 meters long. Admission and visitor facilities may be available; confirm current arrangements with the operator. Wet rocks, deep pools and currents pose risks, and the slide should never be assumed safe to use.

Highlights

  • Grotto at the historic Fazenda da Toca
  • Natural rock slide: assess safety first
  • Private property; admission may be charged
  • Confirm opening and facilities in advance

How to get there

Fazenda da Toca, start of Castelhanos road; privately managed entry

Responsible travel

Avoid the slide and pools when conditions are unsafe; follow the site's staff instructions.
