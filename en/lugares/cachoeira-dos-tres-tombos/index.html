Short three-waterfall trail: beware slippery rocks

Photo: Virto.Photo · Wikimedia Commons

Três Tombos Waterfall

Three waterfalls near Praia da Feiticeira, reached by a short trail. Bathing is possible near the start, but slippery rocks and strong currents demand care. There is no plunge pool at the third waterfall. The State Park requires a guide and offers online booking.

Highlights

  • Short trail with mandatory guide
  • Swim only where conditions permit
  • Watch out for slippery rocks
  • No plunge pool at the third waterfall

How to get there

Official trailhead: end of Alameda dos Guapuruvus, Feiticeira; guide required and online booking (State Park)

Responsible travel

Follow the guide’s instructions; avoid the water when currents are strong or rocks are slippery.
