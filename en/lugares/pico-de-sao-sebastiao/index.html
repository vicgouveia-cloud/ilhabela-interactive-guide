The highest summit in Ilhabela at 4,521 feet (1,378m)

São Sebastião Peak (Pico de São Sebastião)

The crowning summit of the entire island. An advanced mountaineering expedition ascending through pristine cloud forests to stand above the clouds with panoramic vistas of the ocean.

Highlights

  • Highest point on the archipelago (1,378m)
  • High-altitude cloud rainforest & rare orchids
  • Boundless views over the Atlantic ocean
  • The ultimate mountaineering achievement

How to get there

An accredited State Park guide is mandatory
