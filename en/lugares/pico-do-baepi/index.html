Baepi Peak

The Baepi Peak trail starts at the end of Rua Morro da Cruz in Itaguaçu and climbs through Atlantic Forest to the 1,048-meter summit. The State Park lists 7–8 km, a 5.5–6 hour hike, difficult terrain, mandatory guiding and online booking. A lookout about 400 meters from the entrance is separate from reaching the summit. Follow official trail hours and do not plan to descend after dark.

Highlights

  • Mandatory guide and online booking
  • 7–8 km and approximately 5.5–6 hours
  • Early lookout around 400 meters from start
  • Panoramic 1,048-meter summit

How to get there

End of Rua Morro da Cruz, Itaguaçu; guide required

Responsible travel

Book an authorized guide and carry water, suitable shoes and warm clothing. Do not plan a sunset ascent.
