Channel Dolphin & Porpoise Route

The channel of São Sebastião is a protected sanctuary where pods of Bottlenose and Guiana dolphins forage and swim year-round, often riding the boat bow waves.

Highlights

  • Year-round wild dolphin encounters
  • Gentle and family-safe boat cruising
  • Golden hour sunset over the channel
  • Marine photography

How to get there

Speedboat, sailboat or schooner tours

Responsible travel

Never feed wildlife and maintain idle boat speeds near dolphins.
