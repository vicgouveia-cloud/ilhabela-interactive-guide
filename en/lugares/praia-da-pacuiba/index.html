Pacuíba Beach

A small, secluded beach on the far north of the island. Driving ends at the roadside parking area and the final section is reached on foot via a short trail.

Highlights

  • Far north of Ilhabela
  • Short access trail
  • Preserved setting
  • Small secluded beach

How to get there

Parking in the far north; final section on foot only

Responsible travel

Take all waste back with you and stay on the access path.
