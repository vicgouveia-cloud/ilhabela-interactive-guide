Aymoré Steamship Wreck

The steamship Aymoré ran aground in 1920. Today its boilers, shafts, and century-old wreckage form a bustling artificial reef frequented by octopuses, stingrays, and sea turtles.

Highlights

  • Historic 1920 boilers accessible by snorkel
  • Vibrant sponge & coral reef habitat
  • Frequent sea turtle and ray encounters
  • Close and easy coastal access

How to get there

Dive from the beach or an operator’s boat

Responsible travel

Do not touch marine wildlife and leave historic artifacts undisturbed.
